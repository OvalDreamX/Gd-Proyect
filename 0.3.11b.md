## Summary of Changes

1. **FCC Space Mode — AOA-Bounded Vector Following & Retrograde Braking (`scripts/flight_control_computer.gd`, `scripts/repulsorlift_system.gd`, `scripts/ship_flight_controller.gd`):**
   - Active in `LOCAL_SPACE` (`StationLocalSpace`) when `field_state == ONLINE` and `V` (`assistance_enabled`) is **ON**.
   - **Pre-throttle standby invariant:** Arrival coasting with `0%` throttle remains pure zero-force inertial drift (`SPACE STANDBY`) until the pilot touches throttle (`W/S`), space brake (`B`), or braking vector (`E > 95°`).
   - **Speed-setpoint vector following:** `W/S` sets inertial target speed (`0..120 m/s`) along ship forward (`-Z`), while FCC continuously damps perpendicular lateral slip within an AOA-bounded envelope (`18°` target AOA) so turning the nose smoothly curves the flight path marker onto your heading.
   - **Retrograde braking & direct drift:** Hold `B` or pull vector past `95°` (`E`) or throttle back to `0%` for smooth retrograde arrest to `0 m/s`. Press `V` anytime to toggle `SPACE DRIFT // DIRECT` for unassisted Newtonian flight.

2. **Star Wars Low-Orbit Planet Horizon Shader (`scripts/station_local_space_environment.gd`, `data/systems/test_system.json`):**
   - Replaces the faceted `44x22` UV globe + Z-fighting pole-spiked `PlanetAtmosphereRim` with **smooth `48x48` per-face unit cube-spheres** using analytic radial normals (zero pole pinch, zero triangle facets).
   - Adds a GL Compatibility `PlanetGlobeMesh` shader rendering warped warm Tatooine desert striations, terminator scatter, and subtle horizon limb scatter, paired with a `cull_front` additive `PlanetAtmosphereRim` outer Fresnel halo glow.
   - Configures `test_system.json` for **Tatooine (`R = 600,000 m`, `DESERT`)** and places `Orbital Relay Cube 01` in low orbit (`760,000 m`, `52°` inclination, `39°` phase) so the sunlit blue-rimmed curved desert horizon sweeps across the upper-left sky against pitch-black starry space while keeping `OrbitalStationBody` crisp and key-lit.

3. **600 km Planet Scale & Ground-Level Tatooine Atmosphere (`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`, `scripts/planetary_reference_frame.gd`, `scenes/planet_surface.tscn`, `data/celestial/test_planet.tres`, `data/environments/test_planet_surface.tres`):**
   - Enlarges planet radius from `100,000 m` to **`600,000 m`** (`1,200 km` diameter) with **64-bit cancellation-free polar radial mapping** and warped `+Y` cube-sphere topology for seamless zero-jitter collision near Starport Alpha and Horizon Outpost (`~39.9 km` NE).
   - Replaces rusty Mars-orange ground sky & brown terrain with **Tatooine twin-sun desert atmosphere** (bleached cerulean zenith, dusty sandstone-cream horizon haze, golden dune/sandstone terrain), preserves vibrant tropospheric sky during early climb before fading cleanly to space at `45 km`, and upgrades `AtmosphereHalo` to the sunlit `cull_front` Fresnel shader.
