# Space Trucker — Patch 0.3.10 (Simple Solar System Foundation, Star Lighting & Orbital Motion)

**Target:** `Godot 4.7.2 Stable` · `GDScript` · `GL Compatibility`  
**Phase:** Phase 3I — Data-Driven Solar System Foundation  
**Parallel-safety status:** Zero edits to `scenes/ship.tscn`, `scenes/ship_ground_effect.tscn`, `scenes/test_range.tscn`, GLB loaders, anchors, vanes, or `data/ships/*`.

---

## Summary of Changes

1. **Moddable Solar System JSON (`data/systems/test_system.json`):**
   - Defines `sol_prime` (neutral 5800 K star color `[1.0, 0.96, 0.90]`, `light_intensity: 1.0` calibrated 1:1 to solar output), `test_planet` (`TERRESTRIAL`, `radius_m: 100000.0`, moderate `rotation_period_seconds: 1800.0` and `orbital_period_seconds: 7200.0`, `axial_tilt_deg: 12.0`, `starport_latitude_deg: 35.0`), and `station_orbital_cube_01` orbiting `test_planet` (`orbital_radius_m: 180000.0`, `orbital_period_seconds: 900.0`, `orbital_inclination_deg: 12.0`).
   - Calibrated so the game opens at **late afternoon (~18.1° solar elevation)** over Starport Alpha with long crisp shadows transitioning naturally into sunset and night as the planet turns.

2. **Pure Loader & Heliocentric Solver (`scripts/solar_system_loader.gd`):**
   - Loads, validates, and propagates `data/systems/test_system.json` analytically from celestial elapsed time.
   - Computes heliocentric star/planet/station vectors, planet axial spin basis, Starport Alpha surface-local sun direction/elevation/azimuth, and station-relative sun and planet directions.

3. **Continuous + Jump Celestial Clock (`scripts/simulation_clock.gd`):**
   - Preserves `get_time_seconds()` as discrete hyperspace-jump time so all `hyperspace_jump_test.gd` assertions remain exact.
   - Accumulates real-time elapsed seconds in `_process(delta)` (`_realtime_elapsed_seconds`) and exposes `get_total_celestial_time_seconds()` combining continuous real time with additive jump time.

4. **Dual-Scene Solar Wiring (`PlanetSurface` & `StationLocalSpace`):**
   - **`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`:** Drives `Sun` (`DirectionalLight3D`) direction, color, and 1:1 `light_energy` directly from JSON; renders a camera-relative glowing `StarDisc` immune to Floating Origin rebasing; blends afternoon -> golden-hour sunset -> twilight/night sky scatter with altitude-to-space fade.
   - **`scripts/station_local_space_environment.gd`:** Drives `KeyLight` (`DirectionalLight3D`) direction, color, and 1:1 `light_energy` from the live star vector (disabling artificial `RimFillLight` fill); renders camera-relative `StarDisc`; replaces static `DistantPlanetBackdrop` with `OrbitingPlanetVisual`—a vertex-colored globe with atmosphere rim at true angular size (`100 km / 180 km`) spinning on its tilted axis and sweeping along the station orbit.
   - **`scripts/ship_flight_controller.gd` & `scripts/flight_hud.gd`:** Publishes live solar elevation/azimuth, planet spin/orbit angles, station orbit angle, and celestial clock into `F3` diagnostics.
