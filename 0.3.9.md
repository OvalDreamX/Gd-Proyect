# Space Trucker — Patch 0.3.9 (Hotfix: Floating Origin Dwell Gate for Jump Envelope Tests)

**Target:** `Godot 4.7.2 Stable` · `GDScript` · `GL Compatibility`  
**Phase:** Phase 3H Hotfix 2  
**Parallel-safety status:** Zero edits to `scenes/ship.tscn`, `scenes/ship_ground_effect.tscn`, `scenes/test_range.tscn`, GLB loaders, anchors, vanes, or `data/ships/*`.

---

## Root Cause of the `0.3.8a` Failure

`tests/jump_envelope_evaluator_test.gd` loops through `EMPTY`, `HALF_CENTERED`, and `MAX_GROSS`, and on each iteration briefly sets `ship.freeze = false` + `ship.global_position = Vector3(0.0, 12000.0, 0.0)` for **1 physics frame** to clear `StarportPad` contacts before re-freezing at `12,000 m`.

In `0.3.8a`, once the initial `0.25 s` startup grace elapsed, each 1-frame unfreeze at `12,000 m` triggered a Floating Origin shift (`-12,000 m` per iteration -> `-36,000 m` cumulative offset by `MAX_GROSS`). When the test then placed `ship.global_position = Vector3(0, 12000, 0)`, the logical altitude became `48,000 m` (`>= 45,000 m`), causing `MAX_GROSS` at `12,000 m` to report `READY` instead of `BELOW_MIN_ALTITUDE`.

---

## What Changed in 0.3.9

- **`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`:**
  - Adds `ORIGIN_SHIFT_DWELL_FRAMES = 3` (`_origin_dwell_frames`) to `_maybe_shift_floating_origin()`.
  - Resets `_origin_dwell_frames = 0` whenever `_ship.freeze` is `true`, whenever `_ship` is within `1,500 m`, in `notify_arrival_grace()`, and in `reset_to_starport()`.
  - Requires `>= 3` consecutive unfrozen frames beyond `1,500 m` (plus `_origin_shift_grace_remaining <= 0.0`) before rebasing world origin.
  - Result: 1-frame contact-clear test teleports never trigger spurious cumulative origin shifts, while continuous flight and orbital return still rebase cleanly to keep cockpit rendering sub-millimeter sharp.
