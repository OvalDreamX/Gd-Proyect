# Space Trucker — 0.2.5F7A: Geometry-Based Main-Vector Allocation

## Purpose

F7A replaces the prototype's fixed-group main propulsion/lift force calculation
with the existing six-dimensional `VaneActuatorAllocator`. The actuator set is
selected from each vane's `control_weights` and current effective geometry,
not from hard-coded `main_rear`, `bottom_front` or `bottom_rear` identities.

This is the first half of F7. F7B will combine this main-vector demand with
pitch, roll and yaw into one allocator pass so a physical actuator has one
shared bounded output across every simultaneous request.

## Changed

### `scripts/repulsorlift_system.gd`

- Replaced the old main-vector group-authority lookup and direct force-vector
  calculation with a geometry-aware allocation using `lift` and `main_thrust`.
- Collects every enabled actuator that has a non-zero weight in either channel.
  Current effective thrust axis, thermal/damage authority and anchor-derived
  position are supplied to the allocator.
- Endpoint force capacity is computed from the effective actuator authority
  projected onto the requested channel axis. Bidirectional thrust uses the
  absolute projection; misalignment therefore reduces usable channel capacity.
- Preserved the existing authority curve:
  - `0°`: `main_thrust` channel capacity (prototype: 1.50 gross TWR).
  - `90°`: `lift` channel capacity (prototype: 1.10 gross TWR).
  - `0–90°`: linear capacity cross-fade.
  - `90–110°`: lift capacity remains the magnitude reference while the
    `main_thrust` demand reverses to create the braking component.
- The allocator receives the vector's lift and thrust components as simultaneous
  normalized demands. It uses the existing bidirectional scalar output bounds
  and reports achieved force, residual, eligible actuator count and convergence.
- The allocation is solved about the ship datum (`Vector3.ZERO`). The virtual
  actuator forces must form a torque-free resultant about that datum; the
  achieved net force is then submitted once at the hull origin. This preserves
  the established ship-wide main-field behavior and, importantly, preserves the
  real moment produced by an offset cargo COM.
- Added read-only `main_vector_allocator` telemetry and summary fields.
- Kept the maneuver allocator, FCC, maneuver reserve, main-vector input range,
  force application ownership and force-debug arrow structure unchanged.

### `tests/main_vector_allocator_test.gd`

Added focused coverage for:

- Forward thrust direction, 1.50 gross TWR and main-thrust-only recruitment.
- Vertical lift direction, 1.10 gross TWR and the four lift actuators.
- Intermediate 45-degree vector direction and interpolated authority.
- 110-degree lift-plus-rearward braking behavior.
- Linear throttle scaling and zero-throttle cutoff.
- Effective-authority derating from actuator damage.
- Hull-datum application and the expected torque with a displaced COM.
- Main-vector operation after legacy actuator group IDs are renamed, proving
  that this force path now consumes channel metadata rather than group IDs.

## Deliberately deferred to F7B

F7A has two separate allocation passes in a physics tick:

1. Main-vector `lift` + `main_thrust` allocation.
2. Maneuver `pitch` + `roll` + `yaw` allocation.

Both passes read the same effective actuator ratings, but they do not yet share
one combined output bound. A vane configured for both lift and maneuver can
therefore be recruited independently by the two passes during the short-lived
F7A intermediate state. **Do not treat F7A alone as the completed F7 physics
architecture.** F7B will merge all five active channels into one solve and one
bounded scalar output per actuator, while retaining the hull-datum resultant
contract for the main vector.

F7A also intentionally leaves the legacy group-based thermal main-load estimate
and bottom-array maximum-lift telemetry in place. They are not used to generate
main-vector forces anymore; F7B will make those readouts consume actuator
metadata and actual unified allocation results.

## Not changed

- No FCC input/output semantics or bindings.
- No flight tuning, maneuver reserve, damage/thermal coefficients or main-vector
  angle limits.
- No `ship.json`, Blender anchor naming, `.tres` actuator definitions, scenes,
  collision, mass properties, environment or project settings.
- No translation demand or Shift/Ctrl input bindings.
