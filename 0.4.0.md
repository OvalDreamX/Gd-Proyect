# Space Trucker v0.4.0 — Unified 0.2.5 GLB/Allocator + 0.3.11 Space/Celestial Merge

**Target:** Godot 4.7.2 Stable · GL Compatibility · Standard GDScript  
**Status:** Unified main branch release combining `0.2glb-integration` (`0.2.5` F6–F7D & GLB pipeline) with `0.3.11` (Environments, Solar System & Star, Orbiting Planet & Station, Hyperspace Jump, FCC Space Mode, Tiered Integrity & Convective Radiator).

---

## 1. Key Architecture & Merge Highlights

1. **Full 0.2.5 GLB Asset Pipeline & 6-DOF Allocator Integrated**
   - Unified `scenes/ship.tscn`, `scenes/ship_ground_effect.tscn`, `scenes/player.tscn`, and `scenes/test_range.tscn` using `$Visuals/Exterior` (`PlaceholderModel` + `ShipModelMount`), `ShipAnchorIntegration`, `ShipAnimationController`, `ShipInteractionController`, and `RepulsorliftGroundEffectSystem` at child index 8.
   - Merged `data/ships/prototype_freighter/ship.json` preserving all 0.2.5 `ANCHOR_*` names, 6-DOF vane `control_sets` / `control_weights`, and `ground_probe_*` layout alongside 0.3.7 tuned aerodynamic/handling scalars and the `thermal_and_integrity` configuration block.
   - Restored backward-compatible `RepulsorliftSystem.apply_collective_lift()` delegating cleanly through `apply_flight_control_forces()`.

2. **FCC Space Mode Routed Through F7C Unified Actuator Allocator**
   - In orbital space (`SPACE VECTOR` / `SPACE BRAKE` / `SPACE STANDBY`), `FlightControlComputer.calculate_space_assist_command()` computes desired 3D velocity-vectoring & braking force in world space using `RepulsorliftSystem.get_main_rear_effective_authority_twr()`.
   - `RepulsorliftSystem._apply_unified_actuator_allocator()` transforms that demand into ship-local axes (`lift`, `main_thrust`, `translate_x`) and solves it through `VaneActuatorAllocator.allocate()` alongside active rotational commands (`pitch`, `roll`, `yaw`).
   - Longitudinal forward/retrograde thrust executes directly through `main_rear` within live thermal/integrity bounds, while any residual lateral X-slip damping (when no X-axis thrusters are fitted) applies cleanly at the hull center of mass without phantom rotational torque.

3. **Merged Thermal, Convective Radiator & Tiered Integrity**
   - `VaneController` combines 0.2.5 transactional `configure_vanes()` / `VALID_CONTROL_CHANNELS` / `get_vane_effective_thrust_axis_local()` with Phase 3G convective airflow cooling (`air_density`, `airspeed`), stochastic fault injection, startup reliability, and `apply_ship_definition()` live tuning propagation without overwriting GLB-resolved vane positions.

4. **Consolidated Scenes, Tests & Fixtures**
   - Includes binary GLB test fixtures (`tests/assets/ship_model_fixture.glb`, `tests/assets/invalid_ship_model.glb`), all F6–F7D allocator/pipeline tests, and Phase 3A–3J environment, solar system, jump, and FCC Space Mode tests.
