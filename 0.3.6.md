## What was added
- `scripts/simulation_clock.gd` (`SimulationClock` autoload) — Deterministic simulation clock tracking elapsed time (`current_time_seconds`, `jump_count`), advancing strictly once per committed jump and formatting `T+...` readouts for HUD/F3.
- `scripts/fixed_test_route_definition.gd` + `data/routes/route_planet_to_station.tres` + `data/routes/route_station_to_planet.tres` — The two fixed Phase 3 test route legs (`route_planet_to_station` and `route_station_to_planet`, `420 s` simulated jump duration, deterministic arrival transforms & orientations).
- `scripts/jump_calculator.gd` (`JumpCalculator`) — Pure `RefCounted` jump calculator validating route, departure environment type, departure envelope eligibility, arrival time, deterministic transform, and persistent ship state snapshot (no `Node` references).
- `scripts/arrival_resolver.gd` (`ArrivalResolver`) — Resolves safe deterministic destination placement (clearing planet terrain height / station hull extents), restores persistent cargo preset/mass/offset and flight field state, and seats the pilot cleanly in cockpit view.
- `scripts/transition_coordinator.gd` (`TransitionCoordinator` autoload) — Explicit transition state machine (`IDLE -> VALIDATING -> HYPERSPACE -> LOADING -> COMMITTING -> IDLE / FAILED_RECOVERY`) with anti-duplicate jump lock, GL Compatibility radial hyperspace star-streak visual overlay, physics pause during load, scene validation before unloading the active world, and safe failure recovery.
- `tests/hyperspace_jump_test.gd` — Pure + integration suite testing `SimulationClock`, `FixedTestRouteDefinition`, `JumpCalculator`, `ArrivalResolver`, outbound Planet → Station jump (preserving `MAX_GROSS` `55,000 kg` cargo & advancing clock once), duplicate-input lockout, return Station → Planet upper-atmosphere jump, and invalid-route failure recovery.

### Manual desktop check (Godot Editor)
1. Open `res://scenes/planet_surface.tscn` and press **F6**.
2. Board (**F**), start the repulsorlift field (**F**), optionally load cargo (**F4**), take off, and climb past `45,000 m` until the HUD switches to **`JUMP READY — PRESS J TO JUMP`**.
3. Press **J**: watch the radial hyperspace star-streak visual play (~1.6 s) and arrive seated in `res://scenes/station_local_space.tscn` at `(0, 0, 450)` facing the cube station with your cargo & field intact and `SimulationClock` advanced by `+07:00`.
4. Fly in toward the glowing `+Z` docking bay (watch `INSIDE EXCLUSION ZONE` under `250 m` and `DOCKING ZONE REACHED`), then back outside `250 m` until **`JUMP READY — PRESS J TO JUMP`** returns.
5. Press **J** again to perform the return jump: arrive seated in upper planetary space (`46,500 m`, angled toward the starport) and fly the descent back down to Starport Alpha!
