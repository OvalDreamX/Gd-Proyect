## What Changed in 0.3.8

### 1. Closed 360° Spherical Planet World (`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`)
- **Seamless 6-Face Cube-Sphere + High-Detail Starport Basin:** Replaces the flat 9.6 km square terrain patch with a closed 360° spherical `R = 60,000 m` world centered at `(0, -60000, 0)` matching `PlanetaryReferenceFrame`.
- **Watertight Global Heightfield & Seamless Seam Tuck:** Cube-sphere global terrain (`32×32` visual / `20×20` collision per cube face) evaluates a deterministic 3D harmonic continent/ridge heightfield from unit radial direction; the high-detail `4,400 m` Starport Alpha basin (`56×56` visual / `28×28` collision) tucks an `18 m` skirt underneath the cube-sphere border for zero seams and zero Z-fighting.
- **Legacy Surface Contract Preserved:** Retains `calculate_terrain_height_m(x_m, z_m)`, `StarportPad`, `StarportStructures`, `ApproachBeacons`, `reset_to_starport()`, and `seat_pilot_immediately()` so all Phase 3C–3G tests pass unchanged.
- **Horizon Outpost (Stretch Landmark & Navigation Target):** Placed `~23.9 km` northeast on the curved globe (`OUTPOST_DIRECTION = Vector3(0.32, 0.92, -0.22).normalized()`), aligned to the local spherical tangent basis `(east, up, -north)` with its own `OutpostPad` collider, beacon tower, comms dome, and `Label3D`.
- **Orbital Limb `AtmosphereHalo`:** Adds an additive, back-face-culled `SphereMesh` (`R = 62,600 m`) centered on the planet whose alpha fades from `0.0` near ground level to a subtle blue atmospheric limb glow at `>25 km` and orbital jump return (`46,500 m`).
- **Dynamic Camera Far Clip:** Dynamically scales `CockpitCamera` and `ExternalCamera` `far` clip planes across `75,000–400,000 m` with altitude so the curved horizon and full 120 km planet remain visible from `46.5 km` without modifying `ship.tscn`.

### 2. Spherical Arrival Safety & Surface Waypoint Navigation
- **`scripts/arrival_resolver.gd`:** Adds radial spherical terrain clearance (`+25 m` above `get_spherical_surface_point()`) when returning to any coordinate over the spherical globe, while retaining the flat-heightfield fallback.
- **`scripts/ship_flight_controller.gd`, `scripts/flight_hud_overlay.gd`, `scripts/flight_hud.gd`:** Adds **N** key cycling between `HORIZON OUTPOST` and `STARPORT ALPHA`, publishing great-circle surface distance (`km` / `m`) and tangent compass bearing (`000°–359°`) on the HUD and `F3` diagnostics while preserving all `0.3.7` radiator, thermal, cargo, and jump-envelope readouts.

### 3. V0.3 Release Gate Documentation & End-to-End Loop Suite
- **`docs/GDD_ANNEX_v0_3.md`:** Adds Section 18 — V0.3 Implementation Status Table (`IMPLEMENTED / TESTED / PARTIAL / DEFERRED`) and Phase 3H Release Gate summary.
- **`docs/AI_HANDOFF.md` & `docs/TESTING.md`:** Updated for `0.3.8` architecture, full 14-test Phase 2 + 9-test Phase 3 command inventory, and v0.4 handoff notes.
- **`tests/simple_planet_loop_test.gd`:** Verifies cube-sphere watertightness & outward winding, spherical `PlanetaryReferenceFrame` orthogonality & gravity around 360°, `HorizonOutpost` tangent alignment, `AtmosphereHalo` / `Camera3D.far` scaling, radial `ArrivalResolver` safety, and a repeatable `Planet -> Station -> Planet -> reset_to_starport()` loop.
