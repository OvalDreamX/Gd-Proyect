## Why this patch exists
1. **0.3.3 test fix (`planet_surface_environment_test.gd` & spawn height):** In `0.3.3`, `reset_to_starport()` placed the ship at `y = 0.78 m` (`0.03 m` above the pad) and the test waited `await physics_frame` before checking velocity, allowing one physics tick of `9.81 m/s²` gravity (`~0.1635 m/s` downward) to fail `< 0.05`. `ShipSpawnPoint` and `reset_to_starport()` now use `y = 0.755 m` (clean `5 mm` pad clearance), and the test verifies zeroed velocity immediately after reset plus clean post-reset settling after 12 physics ticks.
2. **Horizon & space-climb readability (`planet_surface_environment.gd`):** Expands the low-poly terrain patch from `2,400 m` to `9,600 m` with a smooth outer horizon plateau (zero abrupt square edge cutoff per `GDD_ANNEX_v0_3.md` §5.4) and adds altitude-driven GL Compatibility sky and fog transitions that darken smoothly into deep space as you climb toward `45,000 m`.
3. **Phase 3D departure envelope (`docs/GDD_ANNEX_v0_3.md` §5.2–5.3 & §9):** Adds data-driven departure envelope thresholds to `EnvironmentProfile`, a pure `JumpEnvelopeEvaluator` (`scripts/jump_envelope_evaluator.gd`), runtime `ShipFlightController` integration, cockpit HUD `ENV` / `JUMP READY` + rejection reason + climb progress bar, and `[ ENVIRONMENT & JUMP ENVELOPE (PHASE 3D) ]` diagnostics in `F3`.

## Departure climb pacing & tuning trade-off
Using the existing rated freighter (`30,000 kg` dry, `25,000 kg` max payload, `1.10` bottom VTOL TWR / `1.50` main rear TWR rated at `55,000 kg` gross, `Cd = 0.8`), `test_planet_surface.tres` sets `jump_min_altitude_m = 45000.0` (matching the exoatmospheric vacuum boundary `atmosphere_top_altitude_m = 45,000 m` on `R = 60,000 m`).

Simulated climb times to `45,000 m` at full throttle:
- **EMPTY (`30,000 kg`):** `122.3 s` (~2.0 min) level VTOL (`90°`) / `78.2 s` (~1.3 min) nose-up main rear (`0°`)
- **HALF_CENTERED (`42,500 kg`):** `159.5 s` (~2.7 min) level VTOL (`90°`) / `99.4 s` (~1.7 min) nose-up main rear (`0°`)
- **MAX_GROSS (`55,000 kg`):** `237.1 s` (~4.0 min) level VTOL (`90°`) / `126.1 s` (~2.1 min) nose-up main rear (`0°`)

**Tuning trade-off (vs. provisional 10-minute GDD target):** Because `R = 60,000 m` inverse-square gravity drops to `3.20 m/s²` at `45 km` while constant repulsorlift thrust remains `593.5–809.3 kN`, full-throttle vertical ascent takes ~1.7–4.0 minutes rather than 10 minutes. Keeping `R = 60,000 m` and the Phase 2 vane thrust ratings preserves Phase 2 flight feel and all `0.3.2` profile tests (`environment_profile_test.gd`), while keeping test climbs brisk in desktop playtesting. If a literal 10-minute climb is desired later, increase planet radius + `jump_min_altitude_m` together (e.g., `R = 180 km`, `jump_min_altitude_m = 150 km`) rather than throttling down the Phase 2 vanes.
