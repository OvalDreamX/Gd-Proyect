# Patch 0.4.1 — Configurable Scaled Atmosphere (Jacchia-Roberts), Vacuum Radiator 1.0x Fix, High-Altitude Camera & Test Stabilization

## Summary
- **Configurable Scaled Atmosphere & Optical Sky (`scripts/atmosphere_model.gd`, `scripts/celestial_body_definition.gd`, `scripts/environment_profile.gd`, `scripts/environment_manager.gd`):**
  - Introduces `AtmosphereModel` combining barometric/ISA lower strata with scaled **Jacchia-Roberts** upper-atmosphere rarefaction, **Sutherland dynamic viscosity**, composition-aware speed of sound & Mach number, molecular mean free path, and **Knudsen flow regimes** (`CONTINUUM`, `SLIP`, `TRANSITION`, `FREE_MOLECULAR`, `VACUUM`).
  - Exposes configurable presets (`DESERT_TATOOINE` active, plus `EARTH_STANDARD`, `VENUS_DENSE`, `GAS_GIANT`, `MARS_THIN`, `MOON_VACUUM`) with custom gas & Rayleigh/Mie sky color overrides.
  - Refreshes Tatooine surface optical colors (sunlit cerulean zenith, pale sand-ivory horizon, lighter foreground haze, and tight cobalt orbital limb) while updating `AtmosphericFlightModel` with rarefied drag bridging and full F3 gas/Knudsen telemetry.
- **Radiator 1.0x Vacuum Factor & Early 25–30 km Convective Taper (`scripts/vane_controller.gd`, `scripts/repulsorlift_system.gd`, `scripts/flight_hud_overlay.gd`):**
  - Fixes the high-temperature vacuum radiator bug where high-temp radiative emission inflated `convective_factor` above `1.0x` (`~1.5x` in pitch-black space) by isolating `conv_factor = 1.0 + air_cooling / vacuum_coeff`.
  - Tapers `convective_efficiency` early across `25–30 km` on Tatooine's `45 km` atmosphere so vacuum cooling dominates well before orbital blackness, and updates `FlightHUDOverlay` to switch between `RAD AIR` and `RAD VAC` from convective factor (`> 1.02`).
- **High-Altitude External Camera & Terrain Z-Fighting Fixes (`scripts/ship_camera_controller.gd`, `scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`, `scripts/station_local_space_environment.gd`):**
  - Disables duplicate node physics interpolation (`PHYSICS_INTERPOLATION_MODE_OFF`) on `ExternalCameraRig`, `SpringArm3D`, and `ExternalCamera` while snapping transforms on `toggle_view()`.
  - Clamps camera depth clip ratio (`far / near <= 220,000`, `near >= 0.35`), schedules environment updates at `process_priority = 20`, refines analytical limb-halo normals, and expands `StationLocalSpace` planet backdrop scale to `28 km` with `render_priority = 1`.
- **Godot 4.7.2 Test Fixes & Coverage (`tests/*.gd`):**
  - Fixes strict Variant `type inference` (`:=`) errors across `fcc_space_mode_test.gd`, `planet_surface_environment_test.gd`, and `ship_systems_tuning_test.gd`, adds atomic backdrop transform scaling checks, and introduces `tests/atmosphere_model_test.gd`.
