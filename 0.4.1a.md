# Patch 0.4.1a — High-Altitude Cockpit/External Camera Hotfix, 36 km Pitch-Black Sky, HUD %e Fix & Godot 4.7.2 Test Fixes

## Summary
- **Cockpit Mesh Vanishing at Altitude Fix (`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`):**
  - Isolates high-altitude near-plane clamping strictly to `ExternalCamera` (`cam.name == "ExternalCamera"`) while locking `CockpitCamera.near` to its authored `0.05 m` base across all altitudes so the close cockpit interior mesh never gradually clips away as altitude increases.
- **Third-Person Camera Flashing & Hull Vanishing Fix (`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`, `scripts/ship_camera_controller.gd`):**
  - Schedules `PlanetSurfaceEnvironment` floating-origin checks inside `_physics_process(delta)` at `process_physics_priority = -20` (threshold `4,000 m`, grace `0.35 s`) and calls `force_update_transform()` + recursive `reset_physics_interpolation()` across shifted scene subtrees, ship visuals, and camera rigs.
  - Guards `ShipCameraController._get_interpolated_ship_origin()` against `> 20 m` rebase desyncs and keeps camera logic at `process_priority = 5`, eliminating the ~0.5 s external view jump/mesh flash during high-speed climbs.
- **36 km Pitch-Black Upper Sky Curve (`scripts/atmosphere_model.gd`):**
  - Steepens high-altitude optical column attenuation across `55%–86%` of `top_altitude_m` so Tatooine `36 km` zenith sky drops below `0.02` luminance with crisp star visibility while leaving surface sea-level colors untouched.
- **HUD Scientific Format & Godot 4.7.2 Test Fixes (`scripts/flight_hud.gd`, `scripts/vane_actuator_allocator.gd`, `scripts/repulsorlift_system.gd`, `tests/`):**
  - Replaces unsupported `%.3e` String format specifiers in F3 atmosphere viscosity and mean free path telemetry with `_format_scientific()`.
  - Adds a `5e-6` PGS output deadband in `VaneActuatorAllocator` plus a noise-floor delta check in `RepulsorliftSystem`, restoring exact 2-actuator diagonal maneuver selection in `flight_force_control_test.gd`.
  - Warms test vanes to nominal `60 C` for `ship_systems_tuning_test.gd` integrity-tier TWR checks and extends `fcc_space_mode_test.gd` cold-start integration tick windows (`105` accel / `80` brake).
