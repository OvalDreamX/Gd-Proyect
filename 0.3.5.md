## Phase 3E — Local-Space Cube Station Scene (`docs/GDD_ANNEX_v0_3.md` §1.4, §4.1, §6 & §9)

### What was added
- `scripts/station_definition.gd` (`StationDefinition` `Resource`) & `data/stations/orbital_cube_station.tres` — Data-driven station metadata (`station_orbital_cube_01`, `Orbital Relay Cube 01`, `STATIC_TEST_ANCHOR`, `120 m` cube size, `(0, 0, 450)` safe arrival offset, `250 m` departure exclusion radius).

### Manual desktop check (Godot Editor)
1. Open `res://scenes/station_local_space.tscn` and press **F6**.
2. You spawn seated in the cockpit at `(0, 0, 450)` facing the `120 m` Orbital Relay Cube ahead (`390 m` clear to its `+Z` docking face) in black space with zero gravity and HUD `ENV // ORBITAL STATION LOCAL SPACE (LOCAL_SPACE)`.
3. Start the field (**F**): HUD shows `JUMP READY` (outside the `250 m` station exclusion radius).
4. Hold **Q** toward `0°` main-rear vector and pulse **W** throttle, then glide with **S** back to zero: verify pure inertial drift (velocity holds with zero drag) and watch the HUD transition to `INSIDE EXCLUSION ZONE: <dist> / 250 m` inside `250 m` and `DOCKING ZONE REACHED` at the glowing `+Z` bay frame.
5. Press **Backspace** anytime to reset to the `450 m` arrival point. (**J** outbound/return hyperspace transitions arrive next in `0.3.6`.)
