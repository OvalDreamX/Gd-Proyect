**Parallel-safety status:** Zero edits to `scenes/ship.tscn`, `scenes/ship_ground_effect.tscn`, `scenes/test_range.tscn`, GLB loaders, anchors, vanes, or `data/ships/*`.

## Diagnosis: Why the Cockpit Mesh Shook at Speed

The cockpit shaking was **not** an intentional turbulence or camera-shake effect — there is zero procedural camera-shake code in `ShipCameraController`, `FlightControlComputer`, or `RepulsorliftSystem`.

Standard Godot 4 builds store `Vector3` / `Transform3D` world coordinates in **32-bit floats (`float32`)**:
- Once the ship flies `10–46+ km` away from world origin `(0, 0, 0)` (especially at high speed), `float32` positional precision drops to **2–8 mm steps**.
- Because `Visuals/CockpitInterior` sits only `0.3–1.0 m` from `CockpitCamera`, computing `View * Model` in `float32` cancels those large matching world offsets frame by frame, visibly snapping cockpit vertices against the crisp 2D HUD.
- Missing `physics/common/physics_interpolation=true` and a tight `0.05 m` near plane paired with a `400,000 m` far clip compounded the shimmer at altitude.

## What Changed in 0.3.8a

### 1. Floating Origin + Physics Interpolation + Near/Far Clip Hygiene
- **`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`:** Adds a seamless **Floating Origin** system (`FLOATING_ORIGIN_THRESHOLD_M = 1,500 m`, `ORIGIN_SHIFT_GRACE_SECONDS = 0.25 s`). Whenever an unfrozen ship moves past `1,500 m` from scene `(0, 0, 0)`, all direct `Node3D` children of `PlanetSurface` (`PlanetaryTerrain`, `PlanetaryReferenceFrame`, `AtmosphereHalo`, `StarportPad`, `HorizonOutpost`, `Ship`, `Player`) shift cleanly together by `-ship.global_position`.
  - Relative offsets (gravity vector, spherical altitude, `AtmosphereHalo`, terrain collision, N-key great-circle range & bearing) remain **100% invariant**, while `Ship` and `CockpitCamera` stay within `±1,500 m` of `(0, 0, 0)` where `float32` precision is sub-millimeter.
  - `reset_to_starport()` cleanly restores `_world_origin_offset = Vector3.ZERO`.
  - Raises camera `near` gently to `0.14 m` only in high-altitude / orbital views (`far > 150,000 m`) to tighten Z-buffer precision without clipping the cockpit interior.
- **`project.godot` & `scripts/ship_camera_controller.gd`:** Enables `physics/common/physics_interpolation=true`, reads `_ship.get_global_transform_interpolated().origin` for smooth external camera follow, and resets physics interpolation on origin-shift ticks so there is zero frame-smear.

### 2. Larger Simple Planet (`R = 100,000 m` / `200 km` Diameter)
- **`data/celestial/test_planet.tres`**, **`data/environments/test_planet_surface.tres`**, **`scenes/planet_surface.tscn`**, **`scripts/planet_surface_environment.gd`**:
  - Scales Simple Planet from `60,000 m` radius to **`100,000 m`** radius (`200 km` diameter), `PLANET_CENTER = (0, -100000, 0)`, `AtmosphereHalo = 104,200 m`, and bumps cube-sphere mesh density to `40×40` visual / `24×24` collision per face.
  - Preserves all `0.3.7` altitude thermal curve parameters (`+16 °C` SL -> `-58 °C` tropopause at `14 km` -> `-135 °C` exosphere at `45 km`).
  - `Horizon Outpost` now sits `~39.9 km` northeast on the larger globe.
- **`tests/environment_profile_test.gd` & `tests/environment_manager_test.gd`:** Updated to read `radius_m` dynamically from `test_planet.tres` / `EnvironmentProfile` instead of hardcoding `60,000 m`.

### 3. Reset Flight Controls After Hyperspace Jump
- **`scripts/ship_flight_controller.gd` & `scripts/arrival_resolver.gd`:** Adds `reset_controls_for_arrival(keep_field_online = true)` and invokes it on every hyperspace arrival. Zeroes `field_power_command/output` (`0%`), resets `vector_distribution` to neutral hover (`90°`), zeroes `pitch/roll/yaw` commands, clears FCC integral trim, and arms `_require_neutral_controls`, while keeping the Repulsorlift field `ONLINE` and preserving `fcc_assistance_enabled` and cargo state.
