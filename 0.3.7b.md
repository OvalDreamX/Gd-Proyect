# Space Trucker — Patch 0.3.7b (Zero Hidden Linear Damp, Vane Thermal Defaults & Collective Torque Fix)

**Milestone:** Fixes the final 3 headless test failures from `0.3.7a` AND eliminates Godot's hidden `default_linear_damp = 0.1` wall on `PLANET_SURFACE`, unlocking true altitude-dependent acceleration all the way into thin upper air and space.  
**Target engine:** Godot 4.7.2 Stable · GDScript · GL Compatibility  
**Builds on:** Patch `0.3.7a` (drop-in 6-file hotfix before `0.3.8` Simple Planet)

---

## Fixes included

1. **`scripts/ship_interactable.gd` & `scripts/planetary_gravity_body.gd` — Remove Godot's hidden `0.1` `linear_damp` wall in `PLANET_SURFACE`**
   - Previously, `ShipInteractable._ready()` replaced `angular_damp` with `0.0` while leaving `linear_damp_mode = DAMP_MODE_COMBINE`, and `PlanetaryGravityBody` only forced `linear_damp = 0.0` in `LOCAL_SPACE`. As a result, Godot's built-in `0.1` linear damping imposed a fake `~147 m/s` speed cap (`14.7 / 0.1`) even at `14–45 km` where air density is tiny—explaining why ships couldn't accelerate cleanly as air thinned!
   - Now `ShipInteractable._ready()` and `PlanetaryGravityBody._sync_linear_damping()` enforce `linear_damp_mode = RigidBody3D.DAMP_MODE_REPLACE` and `linear_damp = 0.0` across both `PLANET_SURFACE` and `LOCAL_SPACE`, leaving 100% of atmospheric drag to `AtmosphericFlightModel`.

2. **`tests/ship_systems_tuning_test.gd` — Correct `FlightController` node path**
   - Updated `ship.get_node_or_null("FlightController")` (matching `scenes/ship.tscn`) so `FlightController._physics_process` does not inject unseated cooling ticks during the manual `MAX_GROSS` `14 km` climb test.

3. **`scripts/vane_controller.gd` — Restore Phase 2 standalone `@export` defaults**
   - Restored standalone `VaneController` defaults (`base_online_heat_rate = 3.0`, `heat_rate_coefficient = 22.0`, `heat_load_exponent = 2.0`) so `tests/vane_thermal_model_test.gd` (which instantiates `VaneController.new()` without `ship.json`) warms at `0.35` load within `10–25 s`, while live ships still hydrate the `0.3.7a` radiator curve from `ship.json`.

4. **`scripts/repulsorlift_system.gd` — Per-vane `apply_collective_lift()` force application**
   - Updated `apply_collective_lift(field_online, power_output)` to apply lift through `BOTTOM_FRONT_GROUP` and `BOTTOM_REAR_GROUP` (`_apply_group_force`) at each bottom vane's physical `local_position`, restoring asymmetric damaged-vane roll torque for `tests/vane_physics_integration_test.gd`.
